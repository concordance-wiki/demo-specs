# Integrator

The person who installs the tool and connects it to a forge: creates the configuration repository, declares the sources and their typing rules, sets the pipeline that builds and publishes the site and the lint of every knowledge repository, and does the human steps a forge asks for, the Pages source and the credentials. The [onboarding of a repository](../../processes/ingestion/onboarding-a-repository.md) is their path.
