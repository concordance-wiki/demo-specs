# Author

The person who writes notes in a knowledge repository: a screen, a rule, a term, a decision, in standard markdown with the frontmatter that qualifies it and the links that assert its relations. An author never edits the model or the site; the [lint](../../processes/quality/lint.md) of the merge request tells them what the build will say, and the [to-do page](../../screens/pages/todo-page.md) what is missing.
