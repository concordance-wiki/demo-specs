# Exploring the site

The path a reader follows through the generated site, from a word to the notes that use it and back, without a server and without leaving the browser. Every step is a page of the site, so the process is filed among the screens it walks through.

## Steps

1. Open the [home page](home.md): the search field first, the most cited words as shortcuts under it, then the file tree, the alphabetical index and the freshness of the sources.
2. Type a word in the [search](search.md): the results arrive as the query grows, and a facet on the type, the source, the domain or the application narrows them; the query and the active facets live in the URL.
3. Open a result: the [entity page](entity-page.md) of a note, its type and its highlighted properties above the title, the rendered note at full width, a written link and a recognised word drawn differently in the text.
4. Read the [neighbourhood map](../panels/neighbourhood-map.md) under the note, six entities at one hop, and the list next to it that says the same in text; follow one of them.
5. Open the [mentions panel](../panels/mentions-panel.md) to see who cites the entity: the links authors wrote in one section, the words the tool recognised in the other, grouped by citing file.
6. Land on a [keyword page](keyword-page.md) when the word has no note: its occurrences, files and sources counted, its passages grouped by file, and the same address once someone writes the note.
7. Pin the page from its header to keep it among the [pinned pages](../panels/pinned-pages.md), a row of tabs under the bar that stays from page to page and from one visit to the next; fold the [side panels](../panels/side-panel.md) to give a wide table the room.
8. Come back through the [alphabetical index](alphabetical-index.md) or the [to-do page](todo-page.md), which lists the words that deserve a note.
