# Representation

One of the files an [entity](entity.md) is made of: its note, a slide deck, the PDF produced from it, a transcript, the contract an operation was imported from. A note alone has one; twin [resources](resource.md) merged into one entity carry several, each with its path, its kind and, for a document, its extracted text position by position, and the page shows them one after the other. An imported operation matched by a note lists the contract as a representation next to the note's own file.
