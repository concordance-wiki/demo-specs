# Candidate

Something the build proposes without asserting it, listed under `candidates` of the [model](model.md) for a person to settle: a recurring expression without a note, with its score, counts and contexts, which becomes a keyword page above the publication threshold; a schema an imported contract names, offered as an object without a link; a pair of [resources](../ingestion/resource.md) scored as twins without reaching the merge threshold. A decision on a candidate goes to the lock file of the configuration repository, never into a source.
