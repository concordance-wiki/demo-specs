# Duplicate candidate

Two resources look like forms of the same document, with a reconciliation score between 0.5 and 0.9. They stay separate until a human decides.

Check `W-DUP-CANDIDATE`, severity info by default, documented with a before and after example in the [check pages](https://github.com/concordance-wiki/concordance/blob/main/docs/checks/W-DUP-CANDIDATE.md). The severity can be overridden per project or per repository.
