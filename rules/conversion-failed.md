# Conversion failed

An office document could not be converted to PDF: timeout, size, corruption or missing converter. The document stays downloadable, without preview or extracted text.

Check `W-CONV-FAILED`, severity warning by default, documented with a before and after example in the [check pages](https://github.com/concordance-wiki/concordance/blob/main/docs/checks/W-CONV-FAILED.md). The severity can be overridden per project or per repository.
